# Leave Policy

## 1. Overview
This document outlines the leave entitlements for all full-time employees. Leave balances
are managed through the HR portal and reset annually on January 1st unless otherwise noted.

## 2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual
leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave can be carried
over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave beyond that cap is forfeited on
December 31st.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
Employees receive 12 paid sick days per year for personal illness or medical appointments.

### 3.2 Sick Leave
You get 12 paid sick days per year, which reset every January 1st. Unused sick days do not
carry over to the next year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note
must be submitted to HR through the portal.

## 4. Parental Leave
New parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement) are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental
leave, which can be split across the first 12 months after the child joins the family.
Employees should notify their manager and HR at least 30 days in advance where possible.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family
member, and up to 2 days for extended family. Additional unpaid leave can be arranged with
manager approval.

## 6. Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance, except for sick leave, which can be reported on the day of absence via Slack (#hr-leave)
followed by a portal entry within 24 hours.
