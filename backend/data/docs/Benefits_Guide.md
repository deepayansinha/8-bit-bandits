# Employee Benefits Guide

## 1. Health Insurance
All full-time employees are eligible for company-sponsored health insurance starting on
their first day of employment — there is no waiting period. The company covers 80% of the
premium for the employee and 50% for dependents. Enrollment must be completed within 30
days of your start date through the benefits portal.

## 2. Retirement Plan
Employees are automatically enrolled in the company retirement plan after 90 days of
employment, with a default contribution of 5% of salary. The company matches contributions
up to 4% of salary. You can adjust your contribution rate or opt out anytime through the
benefits portal.

## 3. Wellness Stipend
Every employee receives an annual $500 wellness stipend, usable for gym memberships,
fitness classes, mental health apps, or therapy co-pays. Claim reimbursements through the
expense portal with a receipt.

## 4. Learning & Development
Employees have access to a $1,000 annual learning budget for courses, certifications, books,
or conference tickets relevant to their role. Requests over $300 need manager approval before
purchase.

## 5. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and their immediate family members have free access to confidential counseling
sessions (up to 6 per year) through the company's EAP partner. This can be accessed anytime,
including outside of work hours, via the EAP hotline listed on the benefits portal.

## 6. Perks
- Free lunch on Wednesdays and Fridays (in-office employees)
- $50/month phone and internet reimbursement for remote employees
- Annual team offsite (location varies by team)
