# IT Setup Guide

## 1. Day One Checklist
New hires receive a company laptop on Day 1 during IT orientation, which runs from 9:00 AM
to 10:30 AM in the main office (or via video call for remote hires). You'll need to set up
your work email and VPN before your first standup, which typically happens at 11:00 AM.

Bring a government-issued ID to collect your laptop and building access badge.

## 2. Accounts and Access
Your IT account is created automatically once HR processes your paperwork, usually 1-2
business days before your start date. This includes:
- Company email (Google Workspace)
- Slack workspace access
- VPN credentials
- Access to internal tools (Jira, Confluence, GitHub)

If any of these are missing on Day 1, contact IT via the #it-support Slack channel or
it-helpdesk@company.example.

## 3. Software Installation
Standard laptops come pre-installed with: Slack, Zoom, Google Chrome, VS Code, and the
company VPN client. Department-specific software (e.g. design tools, data science
environments) should be requested through the IT portal at least 3 days before you need it.

## 4. Security Requirements
All employees must enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on their company email within
the first 24 hours. Laptops are encrypted by default and must never be left unattended
in public spaces. Report lost or stolen devices immediately to it-security@company.example.

## 5. Remote Work Setup
Remote employees receive a one-time $300 home office stipend, claimable through the
expense portal within the first 60 days. This can cover items like a monitor, keyboard,
chair, or desk.

## 6. Common Issues
- **VPN not connecting:** Restart the VPN client and confirm you're on a stable network.
  If it persists after 2 attempts, open a ticket in #it-support.
- **Can't access Slack workspace:** Confirm your invite email — check spam folder — then
  contact IT if it's been more than 24 hours since your start date.
