# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Values
We are committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative workplace. This
Code of Conduct applies to all employees, contractors, and interns, both in-office and
remote, across all company channels and events.

## 2. Respect and Inclusion
We do not tolerate discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, age, religion,
disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. This applies to
in-person interactions, Slack messages, emails, and any company-sponsored events.

## 3. Reporting Concerns
Any employee who witnesses or experiences a violation of this Code of Conduct should report
it to HR (hr@company.example) or through the anonymous ethics hotline linked on the HR
portal. All reports are treated confidentially and investigated within 5 business days.

## 4. Communication Standards
- Keep Slack and email communication professional and constructive.
- Disagreements should be addressed directly and respectfully; escalate to a manager if
  unresolved after a direct conversation.
- Avoid CC'ing large groups on sensitive or critical feedback — take it to a 1:1 setting.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interests, or relationships that
could create a conflict of interest with company work. Disclosures should be submitted to
your manager and HR via the conflict-of-interest form on the HR portal.

## 6. Confidentiality
Employees must not share confidential company information — including unreleased product
plans, financial data, or customer information — outside the company, or with employees who
do not have a legitimate business need to know.

## 7. Consequences
Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination, depending on severity, as determined by HR and leadership review.
